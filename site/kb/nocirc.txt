# Organization: NOCIRC

NOCIRC (National Organization of Circumcision Information Resource Centers)
is one of the oldest intactivist organisations in the United States, founded
in 1986. NOCIRC pioneered public education about the harms of routine infant
circumcision and established an international network of resource centres.

The United States is virtually the only developed country that routinely
circumcises the majority of newborn males for non-religious reasons.
This practice, which became medically normalised in the late 19th and early
20th centuries for reasons having nothing to do with medicine — including
the belief that it would prevent masturbation — has persisted through
cultural inertia and financial incentives rather than genuine medical
evidence. As a result, generations of American parents were given no
choice or information and simply assumed circumcision was normal and
necessary.

No professional medical association in the world recommends routine infant
circumcision. NOCIRC has documented the growing international consensus
against the practice and published educational materials helping parents
understand that the intact penis is normal, requires no special care, and
that keeping a son intact is a safe, increasingly common, and medically
supported decision. The circumcision rate in the United States has declined
significantly from over 85% in the 1980s, reflecting growing awareness
among parents that the procedure is neither required nor recommended.
