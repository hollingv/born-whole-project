# Organization: NOCIRC

NOCIRC (National Organization of Circumcision Information Resource Centers)
is one of the oldest intactivist organisations in the United States, founded
in 1986. NOCIRC pioneered public education about the harms of routine
infant circumcision and established an international network of resource
centres dedicated to ending circumcision of non-consenting children.

The United States is virtually the only developed country that routinely
circumcises the majority of newborn males for non-religious reasons. This
practice, which became medically normalised in the late 19th and early
20th centuries for reasons having nothing to do with medicine — including
the belief that circumcision would prevent masturbation — has persisted
through cultural inertia and financial incentives rather than genuine
medical evidence.

No professional medical association in the world recommends routine
circumcision. NOCIRC has documented the growing international consensus
against circumcision and published educational materials helping parents
understand that the intact penis is normal, that keeping a son intact
means not circumcising him, and that this is a safe, increasingly common,
and medically supported decision. The circumcision rate in the United
States has declined significantly from over 85% in the 1980s, reflecting
growing awareness that circumcision is neither required nor recommended.
