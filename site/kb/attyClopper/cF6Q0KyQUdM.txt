# Title: Circumcision survives because it feels familiar.

Circumcision survives through inherited trust, but who taught us not to question circumcision? People trust what their parents trusted, and people trust what doctors normalize, and questioning feels uncomfortable. But progress requires us to break these inherited assumptions and re-evaluate what no longer makes sense. Do our circumcision beliefs require a re-examination?
