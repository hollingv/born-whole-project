# Title: Circumcision is often justified with worst-case scenarios.

The survival of circumcision relies on misleading comparisons. So, what's getting compared incorrectly? The circumcision surgery is often justified by comparing it to some extreme disease scenario that rarely occurs instead of the normal healthy intact penis, which is the experience for the vast majority of uncircumcised men. This exaggeration creates fear and pushes intervention onto parents who are otherwise well-meaning and want to do what's best for their child. But, if we had an honest conversation and an honest comparison, there would be no question you would not remove functional sexual tissue from your child. Should medical comparisons be honest?
