# Title: They say circumcision has health benefits.

They say circumcision has health benefits. Let's talk about what they don't tell you. Circumcision supporters often point to reduce risks of obscure infections, but those benefits are often marginal and preventable through basic hygiene and are massively outweighed by the risks of surgery, the loss of function, and the violation of bodily integrity. No ethical medical procedure would be performed on a non-consenting infant for such minor and preventable risks. We need to stop pretending those purported benefits justify permanent genital alteration and amputation.
