# Title: Why cant I give my newborn baby a tattoo?

Why can't I give my newborn baby the tattoo? We can all agree that a baby can't consent to a permanent change in his or her body. If I tattooed my newborn, people would be outraged. They'd say, "This violates his bodily autonomy." So, why does that principle disappear when we're talking about infant circumcision? Genital cutting is also a permanent, irreversible change to a person, to a child who cannot consent. Follow in comment if you believe children deserve the right to bodily autonomy.
