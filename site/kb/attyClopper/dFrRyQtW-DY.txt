# Title: The foreskin isn't simply extra skin.

Extra skin is an incredible marketing phrase for permanently removing foreskin. There is nothing extra about healthy functional genital tissue. Circumcision permanently alters a newborn's genitals before he has any say in the matter. Claimed health advantages don't turn an elective procedure into necessary medical care. Honest consent starts with honest language about what is being removed and amputated. Should circumcision consent forms explicitly say circumcision removes healthy and functional genital tissue?
