# Title: People seek second opinions before major medical procedures. Why shouldn't infant circumcision?

People get second opinions before undergoing a knee surgery. A permanent procedure on a newborn's genitals deserves at least that much scrutiny. Circumcision removes the foreskin and its protective and sensory function. The fact is, routine removal is not medically necessary for a healthy newborn.
