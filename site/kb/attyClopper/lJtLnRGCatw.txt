# Title: The physical wound may heal. The psychological impact can last for years.

The physical wound may heal, the psychological scar can last a lifetime. Many circumcised men report feelings of betrayal, anger, and loss once they realize what was done to them without their consent. This is not just about physical sensation, it is about identity, autonomy, and the right to an intact body. The trauma of infant circumcision is often dismissed, but its impact on mental well-being >> >> and self-perception is real and deserves to be acknowledged.
