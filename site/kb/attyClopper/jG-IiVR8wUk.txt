# Title: Circumcision is also a business.

Who profits from infant circumcision? Follow the money and you'll find the disturbing truth. Hospitals and doctors make significant revenue, billions with a B, across the United States from performing circumcisions. That financial incentive creates a powerful bias and often overshadows ethics and informed consent. When a procedure is profitable, it becomes much harder to question. We need transparency and we need children's rights to come before hospitals' bottom lines. This is not just about health care, it's about money.
