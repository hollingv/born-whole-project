# Title: Waiting doesnt eliminate a choice. It preserves it.

Waiting does not take choice away. It preserves it. An intact child can later in life choose circumcision for medical, cultural, or personal reasons. A circumcised child cannot choose to recover the original functional tissue that was removed during his circumcision. That irreversible choice belongs to the person who is most affected by it.
