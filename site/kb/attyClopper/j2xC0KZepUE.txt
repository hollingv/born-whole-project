# Title: Calling circumcision parental choice overlooks the person who permanently lives with the result.

Calling circumcision parental choice diminishes the real and permanent genital damage a person must live with for the rest of their life. Parents must make necessary medical decisions for children. However, infant circumcision is fundamentally different. It removes healthy genital tissue for no medical reason and its purported health benefits do not weigh the irreversible loss of healthy genital tissue and the loss of bodily autonomy for that child for life.
