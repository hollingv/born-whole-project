# Title: Its tradition.

It's tradition. Is that really a good enough reason to cut a child's genitals? A lot of harmful practices throughout history were defended as tradition. Slavery, child labor, female subjugation, all were defended because they were traditional. But our understanding of human rights and medical ethics have evolved. Just because something has been done for generations does not make it right, especially when it causes irreversible harm to a person who never consented.
