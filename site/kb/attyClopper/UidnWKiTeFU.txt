# Title: Your body, your choice raises an important question: When does bodily autonomy begin?

Your body, your choice, sounds hollow if your healthy genital tissue can be removed before you can even consent. Infant circumcision is a permanent, non-therapeutic alteration of a child's genitals. Purported health benefits do not make routine circumcision medically necessary for a healthy newborn. Bodily autonomy should mean more than a slogan that's applied after childhood.
