# Title: Routine describes how frequently something happens. It doesn't tell you whether it's necessary.

Routine is one of the most powerful words in the circumcision debate because it minimizes the damage caused by this permanent genital cutting. Infant circumcision removes healthy functional genital tissue from a newborn boy before he can consent. Whatever health benefits are claimed, they do not make routine circumcision medically necessary for a healthy baby boy. Common describes frequency. It does not prove necessity.
