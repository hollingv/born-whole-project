# Title: Circumcision happens before a child can say no.

Circumcision happens in infancy >> >> for one reason. Babies cannot say no. What would happen if circumcision had to wait until adulthood? Adults ask questions about risks, alternatives, functions, sensation, and necessity. Many would refuse entirely if fully informed. That's why circumcision has almost always happened before a child can speak, understand, or defend their own body. This timing is not accidental. The earlier circumcision happens, the less likely the person can resist the circumcision or defend themselves. If circumcision was truly beneficial, it would survive adult informed consent.
