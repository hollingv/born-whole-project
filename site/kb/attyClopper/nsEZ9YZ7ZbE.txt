# Title: The procedure endsbut the impact doesnt.

Circumcision shifts responsibility away from the people who perform the surgery. But who carries the consequences later in life? Once the surgery is done, the hospital moves on. The doctor moves on. The paperwork is closed. But the individual lives with the outcome of the surgery and amputation for the rest of his life. Responsibility often disappears the moment the doctor leaves the room.
