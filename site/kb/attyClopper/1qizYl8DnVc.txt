# Title: Circumcision permanently removes nerve endings.

Circumcision permanently removes nerve endings from the penis. Why is that significant? The foreskin contains fine touch nerve receptors that contribute to natural sensation. Genital cutting eliminates those structures permanently. And once removed, they do not grow back. When we talk about circumcision, we are talking about the irreversible removal of specialized functional sex tissue. If this information isn't widely discussed, that's a problem.
