# Title: For the first time in the U.S., a lawsuit is challenges circumcision on equal protection grounds.

For the first time in United States history, two active lawsuits are challenging circumcision on state equal protection grounds. Our lawsuits, Hader Check v. Oregon and Huff v. Colorado, ask a very simple question. If the law protects girls from non-consensual, medically unnecessary genital cutting, why are boys and intersex children not equally protected? These two cases argue that every child deserves equal protection under the states' respective laws, regardless of sex. And right now, the leading case, Hader Check v. Oregon, is scheduled to go to trial in December of 2026 in Portland, Oregon. Do you think all children deserve the same legal protections against forced genital cutting? Follow me for updates on these two important cases and to see where our fight for children's rights goes.
