# Title: A permanent choice about a newborn is presented during an exhausting moment of a parent's life.

A permanent decision about a baby's genitals is often presented to the parents when they are exhausted, emotional, and barely out of delivery. Infant circumcision removes healthy genital tissue and cannot be undone. Reported health benefits do not make routine circumcision medically necessary for a healthy newborn.
