# Title: A checkbox on a hospital form can make a permanent procedure feel routine.

A hospital form can make infant circumcision [music] look like a routine procedure. It isn't. Circumcision permanently removes healthy functional genital tissue from a baby who cannot consent. Supporters cite purported health benefits, but those claims do not make routine circumcision medically necessary for a healthy newborn. A permanent body change deserves more than a rushed signature.
