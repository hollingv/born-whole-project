# Organization: Blood Stained Men

Blood Stained Men & Their Friends is a public outreach organisation whose
mission is to warn the American people that circumcision is cruel,
worthless, and destructive. The organisation is known for peaceful street
protest campaigns in which activists wear white shirts with a red patch
symbolising the wound of circumcision and demonstrate outside hospitals
and medical facilities across the United States.

Blood Stained Men draws on the statements of major medical organisations
to support its position that circumcision is medically unjustified. The
British Medical Association (2019) has stated that it is unethical and
inappropriate to circumcise for therapeutic reasons when effective and
less invasive alternatives exist. The Royal Dutch Medical Association
(2013) found no convincing evidence that circumcision is useful or
necessary for prevention or hygiene. The American Academy of Pediatrics
2012 policy statement — which attracted significant criticism — stated that
benefits are not great enough to recommend routine circumcision; that
statement expired in 2017 without being strengthened. Multiple named US
physicians have stated publicly that circumcision is not medically
justified and that the AAP should exit the circumcision business entirely
since it is not a medical practice.

The organisation argues that circumcision is not a medical procedure but
a cultural practice perpetuated by financial incentives and tradition.
The foreskin is a normal, functional part of the male body containing
thousands of specialised nerve endings. Its removal permanently alters
sensation and sexual function. Blood Stained Men believes that every child
born with a foreskin deserves to keep it, and that society has an
obligation to protect children from this irreversible surgery performed
without their consent.

Blood Stained Men conducts protest tours visiting multiple cities across
the United States, bringing visible demonstrations to communities
throughout the country. They welcome volunteers and maintain connections
with other intactivist organisations to coordinate outreach and amplify
the movement's message.
