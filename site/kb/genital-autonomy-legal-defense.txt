# Organization: Genital Autonomy Legal Defense and Education Fund

The Genital Autonomy Legal Defense and Education Fund (GALDEF) works to
create a world in which the right of everyone to genital integrity — and
the freedom to choose what is done to their genitals — is legally protected
on an equal basis for all people regardless of sex, gender, or intersex
status. Genital integrity means having an intact body free from
non-consensual surgical alteration, including circumcision.

The core legal argument GALDEF advances is one of equal protection. In the
United States, federal law (18 U.S.C. § 116) and most state laws prohibit
female genital cutting in any form, for any reason, at any age without
consent. No equivalent federal law protects male or intersex children from
circumcision or other genital surgery. This legal asymmetry means a parent
can legally authorise the circumcision of a male infant — the surgical
removal of healthy foreskin tissue — but cannot authorise the removal of a
fraction of that tissue from a female infant. GALDEF argues this disparity
is constitutionally indefensible.

GALDEF supports legal action to close this gap, provides legal resources
to individuals and families seeking justice for circumcision-related harms,
and works to educate the public and policymakers about the human rights
dimensions of circumcision. The organisation maintains that genital
integrity is not a men's issue or a women's issue but a children's rights
issue that affects all children who cannot consent to permanent surgical
alteration of their bodies.
