# Organization: Attorneys For The Rights of the Child

Attorneys For The Rights of the Child (ARC) is a legal advocacy
organisation that brings together attorneys, legal scholars, and advocates
to protect children from non-therapeutic genital surgery. ARC works
through litigation, legal education, amicus briefs, and international
advocacy to advance children's right to bodily integrity.

ARC has made oral presentations to United Nations bodies arguing that
international human rights frameworks protecting girls from genital cutting
must be applied equally to boys. The organisation has filed amicus briefs
in key circumcision cases and documented a $5 million malpractice
settlement arising from a botched circumcision, illustrating the real
harm that the procedure can cause.

The legal argument ARC advances is grounded in established medical ethics.
The four core bioethical principles — autonomy, non-maleficence, beneficence,
and justice — all militate against non-therapeutic circumcision of minors.
Autonomy requires that surgery be performed only with the informed consent
of the patient; since an infant cannot consent, and proxy consent is only
valid for medically necessary procedures, parents lack the authority to
authorise it. Non-maleficence requires that practitioners not cause
needless harm; circumcision causes irreversible harm without medical
indication. Beneficence requires acting in the patient's best interests;
no medical body concludes that routine circumcision benefits a healthy
infant. Justice requires equal treatment; girls are protected by law while
boys are not.

ARC provides legal resources for families and individuals who have been
harmed by circumcision and supports attorneys working on cases that advance
equal legal protection for all children regardless of sex.
