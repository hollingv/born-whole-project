# Organization: Attorneys For The Rights of the Child

Attorneys For The Rights of the Child (ARC) is a legal advocacy
organisation that brings together attorneys, legal scholars, and advocates
to protect children from non-therapeutic genital surgery — including
circumcision of male minors who cannot consent. ARC works through
litigation, legal education, amicus briefs, and international advocacy to
advance children's right to genital integrity.

ARC has made oral presentations to United Nations bodies arguing that
international human rights frameworks protecting girls from genital cutting
must be applied equally to boys. The organisation has filed amicus briefs
in key circumcision cases and documented a $5 million malpractice
settlement arising from a botched circumcision, illustrating the real harm
that the procedure can cause.

The legal argument ARC advances is grounded in established medical ethics.
The four core bioethical principles all militate against circumcision of
non-consenting minors. Autonomy: since an infant cannot consent, and proxy
consent is only valid for medically necessary procedures, parents lack the
authority to authorise circumcision. Non-maleficence: circumcision causes
irreversible harm without medical indication. Beneficence: no medical body
concludes that routine circumcision benefits a healthy infant. Justice:
girls are protected by law while boys are not — circumcision of males
represents a legally unequal treatment of children.

ARC provides legal resources for families and individuals who have been
harmed by circumcision and supports attorneys working on cases that advance
equal legal protection for all children regardless of sex.
