# Organization: Intact Global

Intact Global is a legal advocacy organisation dedicated to protecting all
children from forced, non-religious genital cutting — including circumcision
of male infants who cannot consent. Their primary strategy is funding state
constitutional legal challenges that seek equal legal protection for male
children equivalent to the protections already afforded to female children
under existing law.

Intact Global is currently litigating two active cases. Hadachek v. Oregon
(case 25CV18224, Multnomah Circuit Court, filed March 27, 2025) challenges
Oregon state law for permitting non-therapeutic circumcision of male minors
while prohibiting any form of female genital cutting. Huff v. Colorado
(case 2026CV32110, Denver District Court, filed June 9, 2026) advances the
same constitutional argument in Colorado. Both cases seek a legal ruling
that male children have the same right to genital integrity as female
children.

The legal foundation of these cases rests on three arguments. First, equal
protection: if the law prohibits genital cutting of girls for cultural or
religious reasons, it cannot permit circumcision of boys under the same
circumstances. Second, bodily integrity: children have a fundamental interest
in their own intact bodies that the state has an obligation to protect.
Third, informed consent: an infant cannot consent to an irreversible
procedure, and parents do not have unlimited authority to authorise surgery
on a healthy child.

Intact Global maintains that the outcome of these cases could set national
precedent. A ruling in favour of equal protection would mean that
non-therapeutic circumcision of male minors could no longer be legally
performed in the jurisdictions covered, regardless of religious or cultural
preference. The organisation funds litigation through donations and works
with an Intact Coalition of allied organisations committed to ending
circumcision of non-consenting minors.
